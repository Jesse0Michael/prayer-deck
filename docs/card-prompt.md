# Card image prompt

The prompt used to generate card artwork in the preferred drawing style. Attach
the person's photo as the likeness reference and replace `{{Card.Name}}` with the
card's name. See [art-direction.md](art-direction.md) for the broader direction.

```text
Create a portrait-oriented illustrated prayer card using the attached photo as the likeness reference.
Draw the person as a simple, friendly cartoon character in a homemade pen-and-colored-pencil style. Use bold, slightly uneven black outlines, rounded shapes, and loose scribbled coloring with small gaps of paper showing through.
Construct the face using very simple cartoon marks:
- Small solid black dots for eyes, without detailed irises, eyelids, or realistic eye sockets.
- A small curved line or simple rounded shape for the nose.
- A warm, simply drawn smile, without individually outlined teeth.
- Simple eyebrows and minimal facial detail.
- Hair and facial hair drawn as recognizable shapes with a few loose texture strokes.
Use a slightly oversized head and simplified body proportions. Keep adults recognizable as adults. Convey age primarily through hair color, hairline, and overall appearance, with very few additional facial lines. Keep the same simple cartoon treatment for people of every age.
Preserve recognizable details such as hairstyle, hair color, glasses, facial hair, and the main colors and shapes of their clothing. Simplify clothing graphics and accessories to match the drawing. Translate the person into this cartoon style rather than reproducing the photograph's anatomical detail.
Avoid realistic portrait drawing, detailed facial contours, skin shading, carefully rendered wrinkles, individual hair strands, and intricate clothing folds. The illustration should look like a lovingly drawn cartoon doodle.
For an individual, use a relaxed waist-up pose. For a couple or family, arrange everyone in a compact group using the same cartoon proportions and level of detail, preserving relative heights and recognizable features.
Use a warm off-white background with the exact color #FEF6E4 (RGB 254, 246, 228). Keep this background color flat and uniform across the entire card, without gradients, vignette, or paper texture that changes its color. Confine the colored-pencil texture and scribbled coloring to the people and their clothing.
Place the illustration in the middle-to-lower portion of the card, leaving generous empty space above it and comfortable margins around it. At the top, write "{{Card.Name}}" in large, casual handwritten black lettering.
Keep the background otherwise blank. Show only the flat card artwork, straight on. No tabletop, photographed card mockup, cast shadows, decorative border, or extra text.
```
